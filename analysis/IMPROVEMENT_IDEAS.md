# Ranked improvement ideas

The current export does not establish a betting edge. These are research and operational proposals, not changes to live decision code. Effort: S = a focused change, M = several linked changes, L = a substantial data/model project. Any new trial should freeze its rules before observing future results.

| Rank | Proposal | Evidence | Effort | Out-of-sample test before trust |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Block betting until the model demonstrates incremental skill beyond the market | On 1,898 walk-forward moneyline games, market Brier/log loss were 0.2460/0.6850 versus model 0.2483/0.6909. On 1,835 totals, market was 0.2498/0.6927 versus model 0.2700/0.7484. Every joint-versus-fitted-market paired 90% interval crosses zero. Historical tightened-policy returns, including a quoted +6.4%, are in-sample. | M | Freeze one candidate and a same-time no-vig market baseline. Collect future paper forecasts at a fixed pregame horizon. Require a preregistered minimum sample, improvement in both proper scores with game/day-cluster uncertainty, positive verified CLV, and sensible after-vig returns. Keep bets blocked while evidence is inconclusive; do not automatically promote a model for winning one small sample. |
| 2 | Market-anchor/recalibrate probabilities and permit zero model weight | Full-season 50/50 blending is still worse than market alone. Joint decision-time fits shrink mean OOS candidate edge from 14.0% to 2.2%; 341/1,785 recommendations survive the fixed research rule. Those totals return -6.8%; the +7.0% moneyline result has only 26 bets and a -30.8% to +46.0% interval. Recalibration reduces apparent certainty but does not create proven profit. | M | Use expanding historical training and inner chronological validation for any weight/regularization selection. Lock the chosen fit before the next untouched month or season. Compare market-only, fixed shrinkage, gap-logistic, and simple model Platt candidates using identical as-of prices. Include zero weight as a valid outcome. Require the gate in rank 1; never choose the weight from this report's OOS returns. |
| 3 | Make verified CLV the primary ongoing health metric, with proper scores and ROI alongside it | All 1,096 saved decisions after May 24 prohibit trading; 1,055 have missing price CLV. Missing is not zero. `market_audit_service.py:23` selects a pregame snapshot without a first-pitch cutoff and can fall back to another book; `:54` compares raw implied prices, while totals can also have moved lines. `market_respect_service.py:130` deliberately requires positive available CLV for TRADE. Daily decisions can precede any closing snapshot, and a current pregame quote compared to itself produces zero movement. | M | Freeze immutable entry and latest-before-first-pitch close at the same sportsbook and same market. For totals, preserve line CLV and evaluate price at the same line; separate cross-book fallback from verified coverage. Measure forward mean no-vig probability CLV, its interval, same-book coverage and price-age by market. Never feed an eventual close into an earlier decision. Treat pre-entry movement as a different signal from post-entry CLV. Do not loosen the existing trading gate merely to increase volume. |
| 4 | Preserve immutable forecast, price, feature and policy history | `prediction_service.py:58` updates active forecasts in place, without updating the original creation timestamp. Odds rows are keyed by game/book/stage and upserted. This export has 116 post-start prediction timestamps and 2,161 post-start odds timestamps; 164 legacy edge price fallbacks are unavailable or future. Historical paper mirror creation happened in May for March/April alerts. Thirty-eight actual entry prices remain unverifiable. | M | Append timestamped input/decision records with source update times, model/calibration version, feature hash, original scheduled start, policy version, alert time, book, full two-sided price and line. Store every rejected decision as well. Replay a frozen future interval strictly using information available at each decision timestamp; compare replay output with saved actual decisions. Reject missing lineage instead of filling it with a later record. |
| 5 | Repair the totals distribution and test seasonal adjustments | Totals 0-10% over probabilities averaged 5.5%, but actual overs were 51.6% (31 games). `edge_service.py:667` uses fixed Normal SD=2.5, separate from the simulator. `simulator.py:137` draws independent Poisson scores, and `:163` scales projected scores by 0.82 before June without adjusting the already-computed winner probabilities. Observed season total SD is 4.51 runs and variance 20.30 against mean 8.98. These are unconditional statistics, not a license to replace conditional SD with 4.51. | L | Estimate conditional residual variance and season/park/weather effects from earlier games only. Compare negative-binomial/shared run-environment count models, analytical Poisson baselines, and the current rule. Model integer-line push mass explicitly. Freeze parameters for the next block and evaluate probability reliability, totals proper scores, distribution coverage, and after-vig ROI. Test the 0.82 factor as a preregistered ablation rather than choosing a date/correction from full-season returns. |
| 6 | Refresh confirmed starters/lineups and store their availability times | `pipeline_service.py:81` reads saved game starter IDs and fetches season aggregates; `feature_builder.py:24` has no confirmed batting-lineup input. Missing starter xERA affects 19.9% of home and 20.5% of away prediction rows. Defaults and a late starter replacement can silently change the meaning of a forecast. Historical feature snapshots are absent, so the export cannot prove those inputs were available at prediction time. | L | Capture a new forecast after a confirmed starter or lineup change, using only then-available statistics, and keep the prior forecast. Flag unknown starters/lineups rather than treating defaults as certainty. Compare frozen future ablations (early versus confirmed inputs, xERA versus fallback) at identical quote times; measure missingness and score improvements by subgroup without selecting subgroups after the outcome. |
| 7 | Align odds freshness and model/market comparisons at a fixed decision horizon | Current freshness windows allow open odds up to 180 minutes and pregame odds up to 90 (`odds_service.py:39`). The primary latest-before-start diagnostic can observe prices later than an earlier prediction; it is a fair closing comparison but not an entry opportunity. Some clocks are misleading because of in-place updates. | M | Freeze, for example, a first eligible forecast 30 minutes before first pitch and require a quote no more than 10 minutes old. Preserve postponements and revised starts. Compare same-time model, market and blends, then evaluate their eventual CLV separately. Run a future coverage study before choosing a stricter age threshold or attributing stale quotes to model alpha. |
| 8 | Reduce Monte Carlo sampling noise and quantify repeatability | `simulator.py:137` uses 1,000 independent Poisson draws and no explicit reproducible RNG. At probability 0.5, pure binomial simulation standard error is approximately 1.6 percentage points before subsequent shrinking/blending. The totals decision rule does not use a saved simulation distribution (`sim_totals_json` is empty in all 4,116 exported predictions). Small rank/threshold changes can be numerical noise. | S/M | For frozen inputs, compare repeated seeded runs against analytic Skellam/Poisson probabilities and a higher-simulation benchmark. Measure rank reversals and threshold crossings, not only runtime. Then run the deterministic/new estimator as a future shadow candidate without changing the production gate until score and CLV evidence supports it. |
| 9 | Monitor settlement coverage separately from pricing coverage | Offline production-code replay takes persisted settlement from 36 to 208 rows and repeats with zero new changes. Safe price repair raises verified prices from 11 to 170; 38 remain missing. The reviewer historically covered only 40 alerted predictions, and execution orders were never scheduled for settlement. A high win rate can coexist with incomplete or biased ROI. | S | On future paper data, assert that every saved final game with a supported market settles promptly, while incomplete/conflicting scores remain open. Monitor priced/unpriced counts, stale open age and missing fills. Inject missed scheduler runs and refresh failures into offline tests, retry backfill, and verify no duplicate payout. Audit the first deployed run against immutable entry records before trusting aggregate ROI. |
